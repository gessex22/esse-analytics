Data Deletion Instructions

Effective date: January 1, 2026

Removing EsseAnalytics from your connected accounts

You can revoke EsseAnalytics' access to your TikTok, YouTube, or Meta (Facebook/Instagram) account at any time, directly from that platform:

  • TikTok: TikTok app → Profile → Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions → EsseAnalytics → Remove
  • YouTube / Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions → EsseAnalytics → Remove Access
  • Meta (Facebook/Instagram): Facebook → Settings → Apps and Websites → EsseAnalytics → Remove

Revoking access immediately invalidates the OAuth tokens we hold for that platform; we delete them from our database as soon as we detect the revocation (or immediately, if you disconnect from within the EsseAnalytics app itself).

Requesting full account deletion

To request deletion of your EsseAnalytics account and all associated data (profile, connected-platform tokens, content metadata, and analytics we've stored), email us at gesse.socialmedia@gmail.com from the address associated with your account, with the subject "Data deletion request".

We will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days. We do not retain a copy of your data after processing the request, except where we are legally required to keep minimal records (e.g., billing records, if applicable).

What gets deleted

  • Your account credentials
  • OAuth tokens for TikTok, YouTube, and Meta/Instagram
  • Content metadata (titles, descriptions, transcripts, publishing status) stored on our servers

Note: this does not delete videos already published to your TikTok, YouTube, or Instagram account — those are managed directly through each platform.
